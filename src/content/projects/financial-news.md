## Overview

A data-mining project on Chinese news related to A-share companies. The work covers identifying relevant articles, handling company-name aliases, preparing sentiment labels, and applying a BiLSTM sentiment model.

The later stages organize company and news relationships in Neo4j for knowledge-driven analysis. The repository includes notebooks, selected data, and the project report.
