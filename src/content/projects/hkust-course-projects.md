## Overview

A collection of graduate course experiments in Data Science and Analytics at HKUST(GZ), including DSAA5002, DSAA5020, DSAA5021, and DSAA6100 material.

Topics include association-rule mining, supervised learning, clustering, linear regression, model explanations, web scraping, and distributed computing. The larger news-analysis, forecasting, and MovieLens projects have their own entries in this portfolio.
