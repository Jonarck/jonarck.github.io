## Overview

This website brings research publications, project work, and writing into one personal portfolio. The current refresh uses Astro with structured publication data and Markdown project pages.

Its visual language combines translucent surfaces and restrained geometry with studies of Kyoto Station, the Philips Pavilion, La Tourette, and Hangzhou's National Archives. Responsive layouts, light and dark themes, and searchable project entries support browsing across devices.
