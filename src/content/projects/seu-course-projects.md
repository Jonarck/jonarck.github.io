## Overview

An archive of course experiments and small projects from the School of Artificial Intelligence at Southeast University.

The collection includes computer graphics exercises, automated planning problems, knowledge representation and reasoning, computer vision, natural language processing, embedded systems, and multi-agent work. Implementations use several languages and frameworks, reflecting the breadth of the undergraduate curriculum.

## Browse

The repository groups material by course. Assignment frameworks and reference implementations retain the attribution provided in their respective folders and README.
